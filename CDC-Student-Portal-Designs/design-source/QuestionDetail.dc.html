Longest substring with at most K distinct characters

Topics: Sliding window, Hashing

Given a string s and an integer k, return the length of the longest substring of s that contains at most k distinct characters.

Constraints

  • 1 ≤ |s| ≤ 10^5
  • 0 ≤ k ≤ 26
  • s has lowercase English letters only

Example

Input:  s = "eceba", k = 2
Output: 3
Why:    "ece" is the longest substring with 2 distinct characters
From the students who sat it: 15 test cases, 3 hidden. Two said an O(n²) approach timed out.
